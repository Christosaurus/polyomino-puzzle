Lumen – Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

Lumen is a puzzle game by YOUR NAME, ADDRESS. Contact: YOUR E-MAIL.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, a randomly generated player ID, the player name you choose and an estimated country code (derived from your device's time zone and language – we do not use GPS or your precise location). This data is stored locally and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What is sent to our server

When you finish a round, the app sends your random player ID, your player name (max. 24 characters), your score, the number of cleared lines, the round duration and the country code to our server (hosted by Supabase) to maintain the weekly leaderboard. Your player name, country and score are shown publicly in the leaderboard. Like any server, our hosting provider processes technical connection data such as your IP address in server logs.

What we do not collect

No account, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location data. The app does not use analytics or tracking services.

Advertising

Currently the app contains no advertising. Update this section when ads are added.

Your rights / deletion

You can reset all local data by clearing the app's data. To have your leaderboard entry removed, contact us at YOUR E-MAIL with your player name. You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure and complaint to a supervisory authority under the GDPR.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Was an unseren Server gesendet wird

Am Rundenende sendet die App deine zufällige Spieler-ID, deinen Spielernamen (max. 24 Zeichen), Punktzahl, Anzahl geräumter Reihen, Rundendauer und den Ländercode an unseren Server (gehostet bei Supabase), um die Wochen-Bestenliste zu führen. Spielername, Land und Punktzahl sind in der Bestenliste öffentlich sichtbar. Wie bei jedem Server verarbeitet der Hosting-Anbieter technische Verbindungsdaten wie die IP-Adresse in Server-Logs.

Was wir nicht erheben

Kein Konto, keine E-Mail-Adresse, Telefonnummer, Kontakte, Fotos, kein Mikrofon, keinen genauen Standort. Die App nutzt keine Analyse- oder Tracking-Dienste.
