# Nano Banana — Charakter-Portraits (Mira, Anselm, die Gestalt)

Für die Story-Zeilen im Ergebnis-Fenster und die Cutscenes
(siehe `KONZEPT-lumen.md` §C + Nachtrag).

**Ablage:** `packages/web/public/ui/chars/<name>.webp`
**Format:** 512×512 PNG, **transparenter Hintergrund**, Kopf/Schultern mittig.
Solange eine Datei fehlt, zeigt das Spiel ein Emoji als Platzhalter (🏮 / 🕯 /
◆) — es ist also nichts kaputt, wenn du erst später lieferst.

Gebraucht: **`mira.webp`**, **`anselm.webp`**, **`gestalt.webp`** (die drei
Sprecher der Story).

---

## Mira — die Begleiterin

Junge Glaserin, trägt immer eine Laterne. Sie spricht den Spieler nach
gelösten Fenstern an. Ton: warm, trocken-witzig, ungeduldig, praktisch.

```
Create one character portrait for a cozy mobile puzzle game called "Lumen",
on a plain flat background, head and shoulders, centred, facing slightly to
the viewer's right.

Character: Mira, a young glazier's apprentice. Friendly, practical, a little
impatient. Short dark wavy hair tucked behind one ear, warm brown skin, a
smudge of soot on one cheek, a leather work apron with small glass-cutting
tools, a scarf in warm amber. She holds a small glowing lantern near her
shoulder that lights her face warmly from below-left.

Style: painted "township / candy" mobile-game art — glossy, rounded, chunky
and appealing, thick dark-purple outline (#201356), soft rim light, gentle
cel-shaded volumes, warm inviting expression with a small confident smile.
Not anime, not photoreal, not flat vector — hand-painted casual-game quality.

Colour palette: warm gold #ffc23b and amber #ff9c3d for the lantern light and
highlights; grape purple #5039c6 / #8b6bff in the shadows and outline; aqua
teal #2fd9cf as a small accent on the scarf or a bead. Deep indigo #170f45
for the darkest shadow. Saturated but soft, never neon.

Transparent background, high resolution, crisp clean edges, no text,
no frame, no drop shadow baked in.
```

**Später (für Cutscenes) je zwei Varianten:** derselbe Prompt plus
`…, warm encouraging smile` / `…, worried, brows drawn together` /
`…, surprised, eyes wide`. Ablage als `mira-happy.webp` usw.

## Anselm — der Scherbensammler *(erst für Akt II)*

Der beste Glaser des Tals. Hat die Fenster selbst geschnitten. Kein Bösewicht —
ein Trauernder. Muss beim ersten Anblick **traurig** wirken, nicht bedrohlich.

```
Create one character portrait for a cozy mobile puzzle game called "Lumen",
on a plain flat background, head and shoulders, centred, three-quarter view,
looking slightly down and away.

Character: Meister Anselm, an old master glazier. Weathered kind face, deep
lines, tired eyes, a short white beard, a worn dark-indigo coat with many
small pockets, each holding a shard of glowing glass. A faint cold blue light
comes from the shards in his pockets and underlights him from below. Sad and
dignified, not menacing — a grieving craftsman, not a villain.

Style: painted "township / candy" mobile-game art — glossy, rounded, chunky
and appealing, thick dark-purple outline (#201356), soft rim light, gentle
cel-shaded volumes. Same art style and framing as the companion character
Mira, so the two read as one cast.

Colour palette: deep indigo #170f45 and grape purple #5039c6 dominate; the
shard light is cold aqua #2fd9cf; one small warm gold #ffc23b accent (a ring,
a button) as the last trace of the old light. Muted, cooler than Mira.

Transparent background, high resolution, crisp clean edges, no text, no frame.
```

## Die Gestalt — der Scherbensammler *(Intro + Akt II, bevor die Wendung kommt)*

So sieht der Spieler den Antagonisten, bevor er weiß, dass es Anselm ist. Eine
Silhouette, kein Gesicht. Muss **rätselhaft und ein bisschen unheimlich**
wirken — aber nicht monströs; am Ende ist es ein trauernder alter Mann.

```
Create one character portrait for a cozy mobile puzzle game called "Lumen",
on a plain flat background, head and shoulders, centred, facing away and
slightly turned, so the face stays in shadow — an unreadable silhouette.

Character: a hooded figure in a long dark cloak, seen from behind / three-
quarter-back. The hood and shoulders are a near-black indigo silhouette with
a thin cold rim light. Inside the cloak, dozens of tiny shards of glowing
glass are tucked away and leak a faint aqua light through the seams. One
gloved hand, half-visible, cupped as if holding a mote of light. Mysterious,
quiet, a little eerie — not a monster, not armoured; the same painterly
world as the other characters.

Style: painted "township / candy" mobile-game art — thick dark-purple outline
(#201356), soft rim light, gentle cel-shaded volumes. Same framing and finish
as Mira and Anselm so all three read as one cast.

Colour palette: near-black indigo #0c0930 and #170f45 for the silhouette;
cold aqua #2fd9cf for the leaking shard light and the rim; grape purple
#5039c6 as the mid-shadow. No warm colours at all — the warmth has been
taken. Keep it readable as a shape even at small size.

Transparent background, high resolution, crisp clean edges, no text, no frame.
```
