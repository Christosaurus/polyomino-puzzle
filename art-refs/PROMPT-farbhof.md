# Nano Banana — Region „Der Farbhof"

Vorlage hochladen: **`layout-farbhof.png`**. Je 2–3 Varianten, als PNG zurück.
Dateinamen: `bg-farbhof-nacht.png`, `bg-farbhof-tag.png`

## Prompt A — Nacht (im Schatten)

```
Repaint this rough layout sketch as a polished, hand-painted illustration for a
mobile puzzle game — warm, cozy, storybook style like Township or Gardenscapes:
soft painterly brushwork, rich colours, gentle rim light, friendly and slightly
stylised, no photorealism, no black outlines.

Scene: a small enchanted courtyard at night, still in shadow. On the back wall,
a large round stained-glass window whose coloured glass still glows faintly from
within — jewel reds, ambers, teals, blues, violets. Strings of small coloured
paper lanterns hang across the top, softly lit. A patterned tiled courtyard
floor at the bottom. Terracotta pots of flowers on either side, a low stone
fountain in the middle catching a little moonlight. Ivy climbing the walls.
Colourful even in the dark.

Mood: festive but quiet, magical, waiting — like a courtyard the moment before
a celebration. Deep violet-blue shadow with saturated jewel-tone accents from
the glass and the lanterns.

Keep the composition of the sketch: the stained-glass window and lanterns in the
top strip, the tiled floor, pots and fountain in the bottom strip. The large
rectangular area in the vertical middle must stay calm, simple and darker — a
plain shadowed wall / floor with a soft vignette, almost empty — so game
interface sits on top and stays readable. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI, buttons, cards or people.
Portrait orientation, roughly 2:3, high detail.
```

```
Daytime "awakened" version of this exact courtyard — same composition, same

across the courtyard, all the lanterns glowing, the fountain sparkling, flowers
in full bloom, warm sunlight, a joyful festive feeling.

Same warm, cozy, hand-painted Township-style illustration. Keep the large
central area calmer and slightly darker (a soft warm shadow) so game interface
stays readable. Soft vignette on the edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, buttons or people. Portrait, roughly 2:3.
```
