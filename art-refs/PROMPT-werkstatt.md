# Nano Banana — Region „Die Werkstatt"

Vorlage hochladen: **`layout-werkstatt.png`**. Je 2–3 Varianten, als PNG zurück.
Dateinamen: `bg-werkstatt-nacht.png`, `bg-werkstatt-tag.png`

---

## Prompt A — Nacht (im Schatten)

```
Repaint this rough layout sketch as a polished, hand-painted illustration for a
mobile puzzle game — warm, cozy, storybook style like Township or Gardenscapes:
soft painterly brushwork, rich colours, gentle rim light, friendly and slightly
stylised, no photorealism, no black outlines.

Scene: the inside of a cosy old potting shed / garden workshop at night, still in
shadow. A wooden plank wall, a window in the upper right glowing with warm
amber lamplight from outside. Hanging potted plants and trailing ivy in the top
left. A long wooden workbench across the bottom cluttered with clay flower pots,
glass jars, twine, a watering can, hand tools, a small oil lamp turned low.
Shelves with more jars and seed packets. Warm dust motes floating in the lamp
light. Everything mostly in cool shadow with a few pools of warm light.

Mood: snug, handmade, patient, a little melancholic — a workshop resting for the
night. Deep browns and blue shadow with warm amber highlights from the window
and lamp.

Keep the composition of the sketch: detail in the top strip (window, hanging
plants, shelves) and the bottom strip (workbench, pots, tools). The large
rectangular area in the vertical middle must stay calm, simple and darker — a
plain shadowed wall with a soft vignette, almost empty — so game interface sits
on top and stays readable. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI, buttons, cards or people.
Portrait orientation, roughly 2:3, high detail.
```

## Prompt B — Tag (erwacht)

Lade dein bestes Nacht-Ergebnis mit hoch, dann:

```
Daytime "awakened" version of this exact workshop — same composition, same
camera, same bench, window and shelf positions — but now bright and alive:
warm daylight streaming through the window, the oil lamp lit and glowing, the
potted plants lush and green and flowering, jars catching the light, everything
warm and inviting, tiny sparkles of dust in sunbeams.

Same warm, cozy, hand-painted Township-style illustration. Keep the large
central area calmer and slightly darker (a soft warm shadow) so game interface
stays readable. Soft vignette on the edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, buttons or people. Portrait, roughly 2:3.
```
