# Nano Banana — Region „Der Garten"

Vorlage hochladen: **`layout-garten.png`**. Je 2–3 Varianten, als PNG zurück.
Zwei Versionen: **Nacht** (Prompt A) und **Tag** (Prompt B).

Dateinamen zurück: `bg-garten-nacht.png`, `bg-garten-tag.png`

---

## Prompt A — Nacht (im Schatten)

```
Repaint this rough layout sketch as a polished, hand-painted illustration for a
mobile puzzle game — in the warm, cozy, storybook style of casual games like
Township or Gardenscapes: soft painterly brushwork, rich saturated colours,
gentle rim light, slightly stylised and friendly, no harsh photorealism, no
black outlines.

Scene: an enchanted cottage garden at night, still lying in shadow, lit only by
a large soft moon in the upper right and its cool blue moonlight. A big lush
tree grows from the lower left, its canopy filling the top of the frame, a few
leaves catching silver moonlight. Rolling grass across the bottom with clusters
of garden flowers — bluebells, daisies, poppies, foxgloves — most closed for the
night. A winding pale stone path. A low wooden fence. A few glowing mushrooms
and a small unlit garden lantern. A handful of warm-golden fireflies drift
through the whole scene. One or two moths near the flowers. Two or three birds
gliding as dark silhouettes across the night sky.

Mood: hushed, magical, safe, peaceful — a beautiful garden waiting for the dawn.
Cool teal-and-blue palette with small warm amber accents.

Keep the composition of the sketch: rich detail in the top strip (sky, moon,
tree crown, birds) and in the bottom strip (grass, flowers, path, fence). The
large rectangular area in the vertical middle must stay calm, simple and a bit
darker — softly shadowed grass with a gentle vignette, almost empty — so game
interface can sit on top of it and stay readable. Soft dark vignette on all
four edges.

Do NOT render any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, interface elements,
buttons, cards or people. Portrait orientation, roughly 2:3, high detail.
```

## Prompt B — Tag (erwacht)

Lade zusätzlich dein bestes Nacht-Ergebnis mit hoch, dann:

```
Make a daytime "awakened" version of this exact same garden — same composition,
same camera, same tree, path, fence and flower positions — but now full of
light: a warm golden sunrise instead of the moon, a pink-and-gold sky, sunbeams
through the tree, all the flowers wide open and vivid, lush bright-green grass,
more birds, colourful butterflies replacing the fireflies, dew sparkling.

Same warm, cozy, hand-painted Township-style illustration. Keep the large
central area calmer and slightly darker (a soft warm shadow) so game interface
stays readable on top. Soft vignette on the edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, buttons or people. Portrait, roughly 2:3.
```
