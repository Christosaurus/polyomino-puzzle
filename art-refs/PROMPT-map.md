# Nano Banana — Startseite / Weltkarte

Vorlage hochladen: **`layout-map.png`**. Je 2–3 Varianten, als PNG zurück.
Dateiname: `bg-map.png` (eine reicht; optional `bg-map-hell.png` für „fast alles erleuchtet").

---

## Prompt

```
Repaint this rough layout sketch as a polished, hand-painted illustration for a
mobile puzzle game menu / world map — warm, cozy, storybook style like Township
or Gardenscapes: soft painterly brushwork, rich colours, gentle rim light,
friendly and slightly stylised, no photorealism, no black outlines.

Scene: a gentle hillside at dusk seen from slightly above. A winding stone path
climbs from the bottom of the frame up into the distance, passing three wider
clearings along the way (leave these as calm open circles of ground — they are
where level markers will sit). Small warm lanterns line the path. Rolling green
hills, a few round lush trees on the sides, distant soft mountains and a large
low moon near the horizon. A few fireflies. Mostly cool blue-green dusk with
warm amber lantern light — a world in gentle shadow, inviting, about to wake up.

Keep the composition of the sketch: the winding path roughly down the middle
with its three round clearings, hills and trees to the sides, sky and moon at
the top. Keep the path and its clearings calm and uncluttered so UI can sit on
them. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI, buttons, cards or people.
Portrait orientation, roughly 2:3, high detail.
```
