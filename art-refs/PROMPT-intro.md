# Nano Banana — Intro-Hintergründe (5 Szenen)

Die fünf Vollbild-Hintergründe für die Intro-Cutscene (Erzähler ×3, Mira, Umbra).
Kein Layout-Sketch nötig — das sind neue Kompositionen. **Wichtig:** Szene 1
zuerst erzeugen, dann das Ergebnis als Referenz für Szene 2 + 3 mit hochladen,
damit Kamera, Tal und Häuser exakt gleich bleiben (das Tal verdunkelt sich „an
Ort und Stelle").

**Ablage:** `packages/web/public/bg/` als WebP.
**Format:** Hochformat, ca. **3:4** (z. B. 1200×1600), hohe Auflösung, PNG zurück.
Dateinamen: `bg-intro-1-tal-hell.png` … `bg-intro-5-umbra.png`.

**Kein Text, keine Zahlen, keine Buchstaben, kein UI, keine Logos.**
In Szene 1–4 **keine Figuren** (die Charakter-Portraits liegen separat darüber).
Nur in Szene 5 eine kleine, ferne Silhouette.

Palette in jeden Prompt kopieren:

```
Colour palette — use these together, do not make it monochrome:
- deep twilight violet #1b1450 and #2a1d6b (shadows, backgrounds)
- grape / iris purple #5039c6 and #8b6bff (mid tones)
- warm gold #ffc23b and amber #ff9c3d (light, glow, highlights — the hero accent)
- coral red #ff5b6a (danger / warmth)
- aqua teal #2fd9cf (fresh cool contrast)
- sky blue #45c1ff (calm accent)
- lime green #66e05f (growth)
Balance: cool violet base, then 2–3 warm accents plus one cool jewel accent per
image. Saturated but not neon. Soft inner glow, gentle rim light. Cozy,
candy-like, high quality mobile-game look.
```

## Szene 1 — Das Glimmertal bei Tag *(Erzähler: „…leuchtete von innen heraus")*

**Tag, sonnig, schön** — das Paradies vor dem Fall.

```
A polished hand-painted illustration for a cozy mobile puzzle game — warm,
storybook style like Township or Gardenscapes: soft painterly brushwork, rich
colour, gentle light, slightly stylised, no photorealism, no black outlines.

Scene: a wide view of a small mountain valley on a bright clear day — the
"Glimmertal". Clear blue sky, warm sunlight, lush green hillside. Dozens of
small cottages, glass workshops and market stalls packed along a curving
hillside, steep colourful roofs, a sparkling stream winding through the bottom,
gentle green mountains ringing it in. Flower gardens in bloom, washing lines,
striped market awnings, doves. Every window is filled with beautiful coloured
glass that shimmers with its own warm inner glow even in daylight — like stained
glass catching the sun, jewel-bright, so you can tell the glass itself holds
light. A prosperous, happy, thriving little world.

Mood: idyllic, warm, golden — a perfect summer day, paradise. Sunlit greens and
warm gold dominate, sky blue above, the palette's violet kept only for the
distant mountain shadow. The glowing windows are the hero accent.

Composition: the valley fills the frame, horizon in the upper third. Keep the
vertical middle a touch calmer / softly hazed so a centred caption card stays
readable. Soft vignette on all edges.

[palette block]

No text, letters, numbers, UI, logos, watermarks or people. Portrait, ~3:4,
high detail.
```

## Szene 2 — Die Dunkelheit zieht herein *(Erzähler: „Dann kam die eine Nacht")*

Lade das beste Ergebnis von Szene 1 mit hoch.

```
Same valley, same camera, same cottages and stream as the reference image — but
now the catastrophe. From the mountains on one side, a towering wall of pitch-
black storm cloud pours down into the valley. Roughly half the cottages are
already swallowed in that shadow, their windows gone dead and dark; the other
half still glow gold but weaker, flickering, about to go out. A visible sweeping
line between light and dark cutting across the valley. One thin unnatural crack
of cold coral-red light splitting the black cloud (a spell, not lightning).

Mood: the moment it all goes wrong — dread, loss, motion. Cold indigo and near-
black advancing from one side, retreating warm gold on the other, the single
coral-red fracture as the only sharp accent.

Keep the same composition and the calmer, darker vertical middle for a caption
card. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

[palette block]

No text, letters, numbers, UI or people. Portrait, ~3:4.
```

## Szene 3 — Das Tal im Schatten, Monate später *(Erzähler: „…liegt immer noch im Schatten")*

Lade Szene 1 (oder 2) als Referenz mit hoch.

```
Same valley, same camera, same cottages as the reference image — now months
later, deep night. NOT A SINGLE lit window anywhere. Everything is still
standing but lifeless: cold pale moonlight, thin frost / weeds creeping over
roofs and paths, shutters hanging, the stream a dull grey. Utterly silent and
still. Maybe one single tiny faint ember of gold far away in the distance, so
small it's almost missed — a last seed of hope, optional.

Mood: bleak, quiet, grief that has settled into routine. Deep indigo #1b1450
everywhere, faint aqua-blue moonlight, zero warm gold (except the optional
distant ember).

Keep the same composition and a calmer vertical middle for a centred caption
card. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

[palette block]

No text, letters, numbers, UI or people. Portrait, ~3:4.

## Szene 4 — Der dunkle Garten *(Mira stellt sich vor)*

Neue Komposition, näher dran, Bodennähe.

```
A polished hand-painted illustration for a cozy mobile puzzle game, storybook
style like Gardenscapes: soft painterly brushwork, gentle light, no black
outlines, no photorealism.

Scene: a small overgrown cottage garden at night, seen from near ground level.
Raised vegetable beds gone wild, a crooked wooden fence, a mossy stone path
leading to a cottage wall. Set into that wall, prominent and centred-left, ONE
empty window frame with no glass at all — just the dark leaded frame and black
emptiness behind it. Broken shards of coloured glass half-buried in the soil
below it. Cool moonlight from above. The lower-left of the frame is kept in
soft open shadow (a companion character with a lantern will be composited there
later, so leave room for a warm glow to land).

Mood: melancholy, abandoned, but intimate and not scary — a place that misses
its light. Deep violet shadow, aqua-blue moonlight, a few muffled lime-green
notes in the overgrown plants, a hint of buried jewel colour in the glass
shards.

Composition: the empty window frame is the focal point in the upper-centre-
left; keep the lower third calmer so a character portrait and a speech bubble
sit cleanly on top. Soft dark vignette on all edges.

[palette block]

No text, letters, numbers, UI or people. Portrait, ~3:4.
```

## Szene 5 — Umbras Höhle in der Steinwüste *(Umbra: „Sie nennen mich Umbra")*

Neue Komposition, weit, unheimlich. Hier **eine** kleine ferne Silhouette.

```
A hand-painted illustration for a cozy mobile puzzle game — same painterly
storybook rendering as the other scenes, but this location is deliberately
wrong, cold and eerie.

Scene: high above the valley, on a barren cracked stone plateau — dry badlands,
jagged black rock spires, deep fissures, not a single plant, everything the
colour of ash and old bruise-purple. In the rock face, a huge black cave mouth
like a wound, darker than the night around it, faint cold vapour drifting out.
A small, distant HOODED FIGURE in a long dark cloak stands at the very lip of
the cave, back to us, motionless, looking down. Far, far below at the bottom of
the frame, tiny, a single cottage window glows warm gold — the one light in all
that dark.

Mood: menace, wrongness, a held breath. Near-black indigo #0c0930 and #1b1450
dominate; a thin cold aqua rim light catches the rock edges and the figure's
shoulders; the one distant gold window is the only warm point in the image and
it looks fragile.

Composition: the cave mouth and figure in the upper-centre; the lone gold light
small at the bottom; keep the middle-right calmer for a speech bubble and the
lower-left open for a character portrait. Heavy dark vignette.

[palette block]

No text, letters, numbers, UI or logos. Portrait, ~3:4.
```
